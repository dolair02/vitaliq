## Common Questions

### How do I cancel my subscription?
You can manage your App Store subscriptions in iOS Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions, or visit [apple.com/account/manageSubscriptions](https://apple.com/account/manageSubscriptions).

### How do I restore my purchase?
Open the paywall and tap "Restore Purchases" to re-enable your subscription on a new device.

### I’m having problems with the app
Please email us at **dolair02@gmail.com** with:
- Your device model and iOS version
- A description of the issue
- Screenshots if helpful

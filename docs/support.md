## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
All subscriptions and free trials are managed directly by Apple. You can view, change, or cancel your subscription at any time in **iOS Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions**, or visit [https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions](https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions).

### How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
Open VitalIQ, navigate to the Pro Paywall or Profile screen, and tap **"Restore Purchases"** to re-activate your VitalIQ Pro entitlement linked to your Apple ID.

### Why are my HRV or Sleep metrics not showing?
1. Open the **Apple Health** app on your iPhone.
2. Go to **Sharing > Apps > VitalIQ** and ensure all categories (Heart Rate Variability, Resting Heart Rate, Sleep, Active Energy, and Workouts) are enabled.
3. Wear your Apple Watch to sleep with Sleep Focus enabled so watchOS records overnight HRV and sleep stages.
